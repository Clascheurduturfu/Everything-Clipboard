# Brag Plan: Everything Clipboard

## What is this app?
Everything Clipboard is a secure, cross-platform, self-hostable utility that instantly synchronizes your clipboard across Android, Windows, and macOS devices.

## The angle
Syncing at the speed of thought. A secure, self-hosted clipboard that bridges Mac, Windows, and Android instantly without giving up your data to the cloud.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"One Clipboard. All Your Devices." 
A sleek, bold statement on a dark aesthetic background, immediately establishing it as a premium cross-platform utility.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The instant transfer: showing a copied text bubble moving from one OS to another.
- Privacy focus: the "Encrypted. Zero-Knowledge" glass card.
- Platform support: The orbiting device platforms (Mac, Windows, Android, iOS).

## Outro / punchline
"Sync at the speed of thought."
The project name fading in over the orbiting OS icons.

## User flow worth showing
The core utility flow: A "Text Copied" notification bubble appears on a Mac card → the encrypted text zips across the screen → it arrives and is instantly available on a Windows card.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: smooth, sleek, privacy-focused utility ad
- Interpretation: Clean sliding reveals, polished UI glass-card drops, confident and snappy pacing.

## Format: landscape — 1280x720
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#020617` (slate-950) with blue/purple aura blobs
- Accent: Linear gradient `#2563eb` to `#7c3aed` (blue to purple)
- Text: `#f1f5f9` (slate-100)
- Display font: Geist Sans
- Body font: Geist Mono
- Strongest visual element: The liquid glass cards (`glass-card`) and floating aura bubbles.

## Share copy (draft)
Copy on Mac, paste on Windows. Everything Clipboard syncs your text instantly and securely across all devices.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- Music: warm corporate / clean electronic bed
- Music treatment: fade-in fast, maintain energy, clean resolve at the end.
- Music cue guidance: To be detected at composition time. Target major transitions to downbeats.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS to gently pulse the background aura blobs.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched (clean UI pops, swooshes, clicks).
- Audio-coupled moments: The "Text Copied" bubble appearing, the text transferring between devices.
- Restraint rule: No cartoonish or chaotic sound effects; must sound like a polished OS-level tool.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4s
Text: "One Clipboard." appears centered. Then "All Your Devices." (in gradient) slides in below it.
Visual: Dark background with soft blue/purple aura blobs. The text enters cleanly.
Sequential/interaction: The two lines of text arrive one by one (about 0.8s apart).
Audio intent: Confident opening, establishing the premium feel.
Audio-coupled idea: A clean, deep UI click for each line of text appearing.
Music: Smooth electronic bed.
Transition mood: Slide up → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Transfer Flow — 6s
Text: "Sync at the speed of thought."
Visual: A recreation of the site's glass cards. A "Mac" panel on the left and a "Windows" panel on the right. A "Text Copied" bubble pops up on the Mac side. A glowing data packet (representing encrypted text) zips from Mac to Windows. A "Ready to Paste" bubble pops up on Windows.
Sequential/interaction: yes — Mac bubble appears → text travels → Windows bubble appears.
Audio intent: Fast, seamless, magical.
Audio-coupled idea: A soft pop for the copy, a quick whoosh for the transfer, a satisfying chime for the paste availability.
Music: Continuing upbeat.
Transition mood: Smooth wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Privacy — 4s
Text: "End-to-End Encrypted."
Visual: A single, centered glass card featuring a Shield icon. A "Zero-Knowledge" tag slides in from the side of the shield.
Sequential/interaction: yes — shield appears, then the tag slides out.
Audio intent: Secure, locked-in, trustworthy.
Audio-coupled idea: A crisp, metallic lock or secure click sound as the tag settles.
Transition mood: Crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
Text: "Everything Clipboard"
Visual: The 4 orbiting OS icons (Mac, Windows, iOS, Android) frame the title in the center. The title shines with the blue-to-purple gradient.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: Finality, resolving the ad.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: Final chord fading out.
Transition mood: Fade to black.

**Music mood for this video:** polished corporate electronic
**Audio summary:** A clean, professional soundscape with UI-driven SFX that emphasize speed and security.
