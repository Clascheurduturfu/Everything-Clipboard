# Brag Plan: Everything Clipboard

## What is this app?
Everything Clipboard keeps text you copy available to paste across your devices, with encrypted sync and flexible hosting.

## The angle
Show one real clipboard item traveling from a phone to a Mac. The video opens on the promise from the site, proves it with the real iOS and macOS app screens, then closes with the simple one-time price. The energy is premium and human: useful software, made visible.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The iPhone and Mac app surfaces arrive as one shared workspace. The site headline resolves in two beats: “One Clipboard.” then “All Your Devices.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- The actual iOS screen shows the connected state and a received clipboard item.
- The actual macOS tray menu shows the same sample text from Android, making the phone-to-desktop flow tangible.
- A composed device spread references the real Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS interfaces.

## Outro / punchline
“Everything Clipboard” with “Copy here. Paste anywhere.” and the site's $3 one-time price. End on the platform list and the product's current blue-to-violet identity.

## User flow worth showing
- A clipboard update arrives in the connected iOS app.
- The matching “Last” item appears in the macOS tray menu, ready to paste.
- The app screens fan out to show the same workflow across mobile and desktop.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet, confident product film about a small everyday shortcut.
- Interpretation: Smooth, measured reveals and clean cuts; let the genuine UI carry the proof, with no narration or exaggerated claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds
