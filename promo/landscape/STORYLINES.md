# Storylines — Everything Clipboard promo (Nov 2026)

## A. "Skip the inbox" (BUILT — recommended)
Device: life before → after, then one object across devices. Form: object-to-object chain.
A stranger knows the pain of emailing themselves a Wi-Fi password or a photo; the film opens on that
email stalling, zooms through the Wi-Fi line into a PC Notepad, Ctrl+C, and the same line lands on a
real phone (real adb screen recording). Then a photo does the same trip, then every platform's real
UI assembles into one frame, the line scrambles into ciphertext on its way through the relay, and
the film lands on "$3. Once." and the mark.
First 2 s: light desktop, "Emailing yourself again?" next to a compose window, cursor heading to Send.
Beats: hook (email stalls) → copy text → real phone receives → photo crosses → all devices →
encrypted → $3 once → end card.
Look: light "old way" world, brand night (#020617) for the product, teal flood for privacy.
Sound: near silence + UI clicks for the old way; the 120 BPM bed drops on Ctrl+C.

## B. "The clip's itinerary"
Device: one object across devices. Form: one continuous take: the camera follows one copied photo
PC → relay → phone → Mac → iPad, never cutting. Bolder, but less product UI on screen per second.

## C. "Copy here. Paste there."
Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat — giant kinetic type and colour fields on
every half-beat, with the real phone footage punched in between lines. Best for a 15 s social cut.
