One clipboard. All your devices.

Copy on your PC, paste on your phone. Everything Clipboard syncs text and photos between your Android phone, your Windows PC, your Mac and your iPhone or iPad, end-to-end encrypted with a key only you know.

• Copy text on your computer and it's ready on your phone, and the other way round. No more emailing yourself.
• Photos too: send images up to 16 MB in either direction, with an optional limit on mobile data.
• Encrypted before it leaves your device: AES-256-GCM, using a key you choose. The relay only passes along data it can't read.
• Your relay or ours: use the hosted relay, or run your own server on your network.
• Send from anywhere: use Send Clipboard in Quick Settings, the optional notification action, or share text and images to Everything Clipboard from other apps.
• Background receiving: turn on a visible background session when you want to receive clips without keeping the app open.

One-time purchase, no subscription
Sign in with Google and unlock sync with a single "Lifetime" purchase. The same Google account also works on the Windows and macOS apps and the website, and every future update is included. There are no ads.

Good to know
Android limits clipboard access for apps in the background. Everything Clipboard doesn't use accessibility services to get around that. On Android 15 and later, background data-sync sessions are limited to six hours; open the app again to resume.

Clipboard contents are encrypted, but the relay still sees connection metadata such as your IP address and room identifier. Use a long, unique key and only share it with your own devices. A wss:// relay also protects that metadata with TLS.

You need at least one other device running Everything Clipboard to sync.
